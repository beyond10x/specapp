Layout

Layout nested: 2 part(s), 1 link(s), 2 finding mark(s). 1 dashed box(es) for names that links give and no part has.

  • shore: quay
  • craft: vessel
  • a name no part has

Who guides whom

Layout rows: 3 part(s), 1 link(s), 1 finding mark(s).

  • craft: tug, vessel
  • shore: quay

Quays folded

Layout rows: 2 part(s), 2 link(s), 2 finding mark(s). 1 dashed box(es) for names that links give and no part has. 1 region drawn collapsed: it is one box with the number of parts inside, the links across its border are rolled up by far end and name with a count, and the box links the view of the region and its parts.

  • craft: vessel
  • shore: quay
  • a name no part has
