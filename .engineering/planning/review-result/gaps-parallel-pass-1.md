needs-revision

The three drafts are internally consistent. Their `depends_on` chain is `extends-inherits` → `part-block-aliases` → `cloudspec-warnings` → `hub-badges`, plus `extends-inherits` → `collapsed-regions`. That chain orders only `hub-badges` and `collapsed-regions` among the stories they share files with. Every other collision is either named in a body with no edge or missing from the body entirely, and the planned `scripts/acceptance.sh` rows add a reader-beside-producer gap.

What I read: 3 drafts (`aep plan artifact show`), `aep plan artifact waves --kind story`, `graph`, `list --kind story`, and the scopes of the 15 colliding stories. I also checked that the scope paths exist in the tree.
- Surfaces, by the bodies' `## Surfaces` sections: 3 cited, 0 inferred, 0 unplaced. The CLI scope tags some of those paths as inferred, which is why several collision lines above say inferred.
- Acceptance reads traced to a producer in the set: 2 (the byte-for-byte fixtures for aliases and extends, and the cloudspec blocks and aliases for extends). One of the two has a recording edge (extends-inherits to part-block-aliases).

What I could not establish:
- `fixtures/extends` does not exist yet. `extends-inherits` would create it, and no other story claims it, so there is no collision.
- `aep plan artifact waves` already puts the three drafts after the view stories (waves 11, 13 and 15 against 5 to 10). That ordering comes from scope collisions, not from a recorded edge, and the search stories sit in later waves although they are already active.
- Whether a single `CHANGELOG.md` line is a real conflict is for a person to decide. I rated those findings warning, and the cited `acceptance.sh` and `manifest.rs` ones blocker.
- Whether the split is right belongs to the design critic. Whether the acceptance is checkable belongs to the acceptance critic. I did not assess either.
